MASTER PRODUCT TRANSFORMATION PROMPT
Dot.Forms — Build One of the World's Best Form Creation, Data Collection and Workflow Platforms
1. THE VISION
Let's transform Dot.Forms into an exceptional forms platform.
The current implementation has significant problems:

* Too much generic AI-generated design.
* No clear or distinctive visual identity.
* Poor design consistency.
* Limited functionality.
* Insufficient attention to user experience.
* Limited form-building capabilities.
* An experience that does not feel like a premium, professionally engineered product.

I want us to completely rethink what Dot.Forms should be.
The ambition is to build a forms platform that can compete with, outperform and look better than the leading form-building platforms available today.
Study products such as:

* Typeform — conversational forms and engaging user experiences.
* Jotform — broad form-building functionality and templates.
* Tally — simplicity and effortless form creation.
* Fillout — advanced forms and integrations.
* Google Forms — accessibility and ease of use.
* Microsoft Forms — business and organisational workflows.
* Paperform — visually rich form experiences.
* Formstack — enterprise form and workflow capabilities.
* Cognito Forms — advanced form logic and calculations.
* SurveyMonkey — surveys, research and response analysis.

These products are references for identifying excellent functionality and usability, not templates to copy.
Dot.Forms must develop its own identity, its own interaction philosophy and its own distinctive experience.
The objective is not to build another form builder. It is to create an intelligent platform that makes collecting information, designing experiences, automating processes and understanding responses exceptionally easy.
I want users to open Dot.Forms and immediately feel that they are using something thoughtfully designed, powerful, modern and different.

PART E — ADVANCED FORM LOGIC AND INTELLIGENCE

Allow users to create rules such as:

* If the answer is X, show question Y.
* If the answer is X, hide section Y.
* If a user selects a particular option, redirect to a relevant page.
* If a value exceeds a threshold, trigger an approval workflow.
* If a user chooses a specific answer, request supporting documents.

Support:

* AND conditions.
* OR conditions.
* Multiple rules.
* Nested conditions where appropriate.
* Field-based conditions.
* Value comparisons.
* Empty and non-empty conditions.
* Conditional page navigation.
* Conditional required fields.

PART I — AUTOMATION AND WORKFLOWS
23. Turn Forms into Business Processes
Dot.Forms should go beyond collecting information.
It should help businesses turn submissions into actions.
Examples:
Leave Application
Employee submits a form → manager receives notification → manager approves or rejects → employee receives the outcome → relevant records are updated through an authorised integration.
Purchase Request
Employee submits a purchase request → relevant manager reviews → finance approves → the requester receives an update → approved information is sent to the appropriate financial workflow.
Customer Enquiry
Customer submits an enquiry → lead is created → responsible person is notified → follow-up is tracked.
Incident Report
Employee submits an incident report → responsible department is notified → investigation is initiated → status is tracked.
Supplier Registration
Supplier submits details → documents are collected → verification and review take place → approval status is communicated.
Support configurable workflow stages.
Do not assume every submission should automatically trigger an action.
Allow administrators to define approval requirements, responsible people and notification rules.
24. Workflow Builder

* Submission received.

* Request approval.

* Send confirmation.

Ensure workflows are idempotent and cannot accidentally create duplicate business records or payments.

FINAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
Dot.Forms must follow these principles:
1. Beautiful by default
Users should create professional forms without needing design skills.
2. Effortless for beginners
A first-time user should be able to create and publish a form within minutes.
3. Powerful for professionals
Advanced users should have access to sophisticated logic, calculations, workflows and analytics.
4. Intelligent, not gimmicky
AI must save time and improve outcomes rather than exist as decoration.
5. Flexible without being complicated
Advanced functionality should not make everyday tasks difficult.
6. Reliable with real data
Forms, submissions, attachments, workflows and analytics must work with actual application data.
7. Enterprise-ready
Security, permissions, scalability, governance and tenant isolation must be part of the architecture from the beginning.
8. Distinctive
Dot.Forms must have a recognisable product identity and a polished experience that does not look like a generic form builder.
9. User-centred
Every interaction must make sense. The product should guide users rather than force them to figure everything out.
10. Continuously improvable
Build an architecture that allows new field types, integrations, templates and workflow capabilities to be introduced without constantly rebuilding the platform.
